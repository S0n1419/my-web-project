# 🌾 GIEO - Agriculture E-commerce Website

Welcome to **GIEO** – a website dedicated to delivering fresh, high-quality agricultural products to your doorstep. 🌿🍎🥬  
Our mission is to **connect people with nature** by bringing the best from Vietnamese farms directly to customers in the most convenient way.

---

## 💡 About the Project

This website is developed as part of a collaborative project between students from:

- 🎓 **National Economics University**
- 🎓 **Diplomatic Academy of Vietnam**
- 🎓 **Ha Noi University of Science and Technology**

We, **Team GIEO**, are passionate about agriculture, sustainability, and digital transformation. This platform reflects our goal of combining **technology and agriculture** to benefit both local farmers and consumers.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Team Introduction**: Learn more about us – who we are and why we do what we do.
- 🍓 **Product Showcase**: Explore a variety of agricultural products, including fruits, vegetables, and more.
- 🛒 **Online Shopping**: Add products to your cart and purchase them with ease.
- 📞 **Contact Us**: Reach out directly with questions, suggestions, or partnerships.

---

## 🌱 Our Values

At GIEO, we believe in:
- **Transparency** in sourcing and pricing
- **Sustainability** in farming and packaging
- **Community** — supporting local farmers and healthy lifestyles
